# Future Interns - Data Science & Analytics Track
## Task 2: Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

### 📌 Project Overview
This project involves analyzing customer retention and churn patterns for a subscription-based telecommunications service. The objective is to evaluate attrition drivers across contract terms, internet service types, demographic segments, and payment methods to provide actionable retention strategies.


### 📊 Dashboard Preview
![Dashboard Preview](Task2_Dashboard_Screenshot.png)


### 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- *Total Customers:* 7,043
- *Churned Customers:* 1,869
- *Overall Churn Rate:* 26.54%


### 🔍 Analytical Insights
1. *Contract Type Risk:* Customers on month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate at *42.71%, compared to **11.27%* for one-year and *2.83%* for two-year contracts.
2. *Service Vulnerability:* Fiber optic internet subscribers exhibit a significantly higher churn rate (*41.89%) than DSL users (18.96%*), pointing towards potential price sensitivity or service quality challenges.
3. *Payment Method Attrition:* Electronic check payments show the highest customer attrition (~45%), whereas automated options (Bank transfer and Credit card) demonstrate much stronger retention.


### 💡 Business Recommendations
- *Contract Migration:* Offer loyalty incentives and discounts to encourage short-term (month-to-month) customers to sign annual contracts.
- *Service Quality Review:* Investigate technical support and network reliability tickets for fiber optic subscribers to mitigate churn.
- *Auto-Pay Adoption:* Incentivize automated billing options through one-time statement credits.
